Course learning outcomes

What will learners achieve?

By the end of this learning programme, students and research scholars should be able to:

  1. Explain the meaning, scope, approaches and stages of research methodology.
  2. Formulate a focused research problem, objectives, research questions and a literature-based rationale.
  3. Design a coherent research plan and justify the selection of methods, sampling procedures and measurement tools.
  4. Collect and prepare primary or secondary data using appropriate procedures and ethical safeguards.
  5. Apply and interpret suitable descriptive and inferential statistical techniques, recognising their assumptions and limitations.
  6. Communicate research findings through a structured report with transparent interpretation, citations and limitations.
  7. Use digital tools responsibly for literature management, data processing, analysis and research communication.

Unit-wise learning outcomes

What does each unit help you achieve?

The 15 chapters are grouped below into five teaching units for a clear learning progression. These are instructional groupings created for this website, not a claim that the book itself prescribes these exact units.

UnitChapters coveredLearning achievement
Unit I
Foundations of Research		Chapters 1–3Explain research concepts and approaches; identify and define a research problem; develop objectives and questions; select and justify an appropriate research design.
Unit II
Sampling, Measurement and Data Collection		Chapters 4–6Define population, sampling frame and sampling unit; compare probability and non-probability methods; operationalise constructs; select measurement scales and appropriate data-collection methods.
Unit III
Data Processing, Analysis and Estimation		Chapters 7–8Organise, code and clean data; apply descriptive analysis; understand sampling distributions, standard errors, estimation and confidence intervals; interpret uncertainty responsibly.
Unit IV
Statistical Testing and Multivariate Techniques		Chapters 9–13Formulate and test hypotheses; choose and interpret parametric, chi-square and non-parametric tests; understand ANOVA/ANCOVA and select suitable multivariate techniques based on research objectives and assumptions.
Unit V
Interpretation, Reporting and Digital Research		Chapters 14–15Interpret findings in relation to objectives and literature; recognise limitations; prepare a structured research report; use digital tools responsibly for research, analysis and communication.
एककनिहाय अध्ययन निष्पत्ती: Unit I मध्ये संशोधनाची पायाभरणी, समस्या निश्चिती आणि संशोधन आराखडा; Unit II मध्ये नमुना निवड, मापन आणि माहिती संकलन; Unit III मध्ये माहिती प्रक्रिया, विश्लेषण आणि अनुमान; Unit IV मध्ये परिकल्पना परीक्षण व सांख्यिकीय तंत्रे; तर Unit V मध्ये निष्कर्षांचे अर्थनिर्वचन, अहवाल लेखन आणि डिजिटल साधनांचा जबाबदार वापर साध्य होईल.
